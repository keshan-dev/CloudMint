Privacy Policy &
Data Processing Practices

Effective Date: September 26, 2026. Learn how CloudMint and our AI assistant handle information.

Mandatory AI Assistant Warning

CloudMint includes an AI-powered assistant. Messages submitted to the assistant are processed to generate responses. Do not submit passwords, payment information, authentication codes, or other sensitive personal information.

1. Overview and Scope

CloudMint, Inc. ("CloudMint", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy describes our practices regarding information collected through our website (cloudmint-demo.pages.dev), our customer contact forms, and our interactive CloudMint Assistant chatbot.

2. Data Collected by the Site

We collect only minimal information necessary to deliver, protect, and optimize our services:

  • Technical & Telemetry Data: Client IP address (masked in logs), browser user-agent, request timestamps, and referring URLs collected at the edge for rate limiting and bot mitigation.
  • Contact Submissions: Your name, work email address, and inquiry message when you submit the contact form.
  • Local Storage: We use browser localStorage solely to persist your telemetry consent choice. No tracking cookies or cross-site tracking scripts are loaded.

3. AI Assistant Data Processing

When you interact with the CloudMint Assistant, your messages and short-term dialogue context are transmitted to our serverless endpoint (/api/chat) hosted on Cloudflare.

  • Inference Provider: Inference is executed using Cloudflare Workers AI. Cloudflare operates under strict data-protection agreements and does not retain your prompts to train foundation models.
  • Retention Policy: Chat conversations are maintained in-memory only during your active session. We do not store persistent chat history in local storage or permanent databases.
  • Verification Tokens: To prevent denial-of-service abuse, interaction tokens generated by Cloudflare Turnstile are verified server-side. Turnstile does not track users across websites.

4. Third-Party Infrastructure Services

CloudMint utilizes Cloudflare, Inc. for edge hosting (Cloudflare Pages), DNS, web application firewall (WAF), Bot Fight Mode, and serverless AI compute. All network traffic between your client and CloudMint is encrypted via TLS 1.3.

5. User Rights & Contact Inquiries

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, wish to request deletion of contact form submissions, or need security disclosures, contact our privacy officer at support@cloudmint.io.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically to reflect changes in our technology or legal requirements. Material updates will be indicated by revising the effective date at the top of this page.
