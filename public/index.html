CloudMint is a high-performance cloud orchestration platform that simplifies multi-cloud storage, automates edge distribution, and provides unified developer APIs across heterogeneous infrastructure.

CloudMint offers two simple plans: the Starter Plan at $19/month (includes 100 GB storage and 5 edge nodes) and the Pro Plan at $79/month (includes unlimited storage, custom domain routing, and priority 24/7 support).

CloudMint natively supports REST API, gRPC, and WebSockets for real-time streaming and telemetry pipelines.

All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256. CloudMint enforces zero-trust token authentication and never trains artificial intelligence models on your data.
